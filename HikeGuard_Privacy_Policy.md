## English

**HikeGuard — Privacy Policy**

Last updated: [DATE]

HikeGuard ("the app") is an iPhone hiking application developed by Yann Crausaz ("we", "us", "the developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and the choices you have.

**Short version:** HikeGuard is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your hikes, we do not require an account, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. Your recorded tracks stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share them.

**Information the app processes**

- **Location data.** When you record a hike, the app uses your iPhone's GPS to record your position (coordinates, timestamps and elevation). Location is used to draw your recorded track, calculate distance travelled and cumulative ascent, show your current position on the map and — when you load a reference route — measure how far you are from that route so it can alert you if you leave it. With your permission, the app uses location in the background so recording continues while your screen is locked or another app is open.
- **Motion and barometric data.** On iPhones equipped with a barometer, the app reads relative altitude changes from the device's motion and barometer sensors to calculate cumulative ascent more accurately than GPS altitude alone. This data is processed on your device.
- **Route and track files.** You may import GPX or KML files as reference routes, and you can export your recorded hike as a GPX file. These files are handled on your device and are only shared when you choose to share them.

**How your information is used**

The app uses the data above solely to provide its features: recording your hike, showing maps and your position, tracking elevation and distance, following a reference route, and alerting you when you leave it. The app does not use your data for advertising, profiling or tracking across other apps or websites.

**Data stays on your device**

Your recorded hikes, statistics, event logs and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, receive or store this information on any server and has no access to your hikes.

**Third-party map providers**

To display maps, the app requests map tiles from the map provider you select in Settings: Apple Maps, SwissTopo (Swiss Federal Office of Topography swisstopo) or OpenTopoMap. When a map is displayed, the selected provider necessarily receives the map area being requested in order to return the correct tiles. These providers process such requests under their own privacy policies:

- Apple Maps — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- swisstopo — https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch
- OpenTopoMap — https://opentopomap.org/about

We do not control and are not responsible for the practices of these providers.

**Notifications and Live Activities**

The app uses local notifications and Live Activities to show information and controls (such as off-route alerts and hiking progress) while you hike, including when the app is not on screen. These are generated on your device; no push server is involved.

**Sharing your data**

The app does not share your data with us or with any third party, except: (a) with the map providers described above, to display maps; and (b) when you choose to export or share a GPX file (for example via the iOS Files app, AirDrop, Mail, Messages or another app you select). In that case the data goes wherever you send it, and that destination's own terms and privacy policy apply.

**Data retention and deletion**

Because your data is stored on your device, you remain in control of it. You can delete individual hiking sessions in the app, and deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Files you have already exported or shared are not affected by deleting the app.

**Children's privacy**

HikeGuard is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Security**

Your data is stored within the app's own storage on your iPhone and benefits from the security features of iOS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but because your hikes are not transmitted to us, they are not exposed to a breach on our side.

**Your rights**

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), including the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Because HikeGuard stores your data only on your device and we do not hold it, you can exercise these rights directly by viewing, editing or deleting your data in the app. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

**Changes to this policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app or on this page.

**Contact**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: Yann Crausaz — [your contact email]

Ces fournisseurs traitent ces demandes selon leurs propres politiques de confidentialité :

HikeGuard ne s'adresse pas aux enfants et ne collecte pas sciemment de données personnelles les concernant.

**Sécurité**

Vos données sont stockées dans l'espace de stockage propre à l'app sur votre iPhone et bénéficient des fonctions de sécurité d'iOS. Aucune méthode de stockage ou de transmission électronique n'est totalement sûre, mais comme vos randonnées ne nous sont pas transmises, elles ne sont pas exposées à une faille de notre côté.
